Common Questions

Which cities does MoveX serve?+
MoveX currently serves Jaipur and surrounding areas. We are rapidly expanding to more cities across Rajasthan and India. Contact us to check availability in your city.
What vehicles are available for booking?+
We offer Tata Ace, Pickup Trucks, Tempos, and larger vehicles as per your requirement. All vehicles are GPS tracked and driven by trained, verified drivers.
How quickly can I get a truck?+
After submitting your booking request, our team will call you within 2 hours to confirm your booking. Same-day pickups are available based on fleet availability.
Is my cargo insured during transit?+
Absolutely! We take full responsibility for your goods during transport. Our drivers are trained, vehicles are well-maintained, and we handle every shipment with maximum care. Your cargo is in safe hands with MoveX.
Can I track my shipment in real time?+
Yes! All our trucks are GPS-enabled. You will receive real-time location updates via WhatsApp. Our support team keeps you informed at every step of the journey.
Do you offer monthly contract services?+
Yes, we offer monthly and annual contract transport services for businesses with regular shipping needs. Contract clients get priority trucks, fixed pricing, and a dedicated account manager.

Our Story

Built by Logistics Experts, for Business

MoveX was founded with one mission — to make truck booking as easy as booking a cab. Before MoveX, finding a reliable truck meant hours of calls, price negotiations, and uncertainty. We changed that.

Starting in Jaipur in 2026, we built a platform where anyone can book a mini truck, tempo, or pickup van in minutes — with real-time tracking, verified drivers, and guaranteed on-time delivery.

Today, MoveX serves hundreds of customers — shopkeepers, homeowners, factories, and e-commerce sellers — all relying on us for their daily logistics needs.
